> Last Updated: December 7, 2025

## The Problem

**The global tango community is fragmented and underserved by technology.**

- 50+ million tango dancers worldwide across 150+ countries
- No unified platform for events, connections, or travel
- Facebook groups are chaotic; local apps are siloed
- Teachers and organizers lack professional tools
- Cross-border community building is nearly impossible

---

## Our Solution

**Mundo Tango: The operating system for the global tango community.**

A social platform purpose-built for tango dancers that combines:
- **Event Discovery** - Find milongas, workshops, and festivals anywhere
- **Social Connection** - Friend dancers, follow teachers, join city groups
- **AI Assistant** - Mr. Blue helps with travel planning and partner matching
- **Professional Tools** - For teachers, DJs, and organizers to grow their business

## Target Segment

### Primary: Active Tango Dancers (Beachhead)
- Dance 2+ times/week
- Travel for tango festivals
- 25-55 age range, global
- High spending on events, travel, lessons

### Secondary: Tango Professionals
- Teachers, DJs, organizers
- Need tools for marketing, scheduling, payments
- Willing to pay for premium features

### Tertiary: Curious Beginners
- Want to start tango
- Need discovery and onboarding
- Future community growth

---

## Business Model

### Revenue Streams

1. **Premium Subscriptions** ($10-25/month)
   - Advanced matching, unlimited messaging
   - Priority event listings
   - Professional profile features

2. **Transaction Fees** (3-5%)
   - Event ticket sales
   - Lesson bookings
   - Marketplace transactions

3. **Promoted Listings**
   - Featured events and teachers
   - City sponsorships
   - Festival partnerships

4. **Enterprise/B2B**
   - White-label for large festivals
   - API access for tango schools
   - Data insights for organizers
