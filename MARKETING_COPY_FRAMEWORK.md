Headline: Stay With Fellow Dancers
Body: Find affordable, tango-friendly accommodations wherever you dance. From festival weekends to long-term stays.

#### Community Tribes
Headline: Find Your Tribe  
Body: Join communities based on your interests—traditional tango, nuevo, teaching, organizing. Connect with like-minded dancers.

Headline: Grow Your Tango Career
Body: Connect with teachers, DJs, organizers, performers. Build your network, showcase your work, discover opportunities.

#### Messaging
Headline: Stay Connected
Body: Message other dancers, coordinate meetups, discuss events, and keep the conversation going between milongas.
