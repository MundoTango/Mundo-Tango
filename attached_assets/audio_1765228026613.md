# Is it better and cheaper to use eleven labs or grok?

For what you want (everyone clicks a mic and talks to Mr Blue), ElevenLabs plus an LLM (which can be Grok) is the better fit; Grok alone does not replace ElevenLabs’ real‑time voice stack, while ElevenLabs does not replace a general reasoning LLM.[^3_1][^3_2]

## Cost picture

- ElevenLabs Agents is priced roughly per minute of audio (around 0.08–0.10 USD per active conversation minute on business/scale tiers, with cheaper or free starter tiers and heavy discounts during silence).[^3_3][^3_4][^3_1]
- Grok’s API is priced per million tokens (for example, Grok‑4 is around 3 USD per 1M input tokens and 15 USD per 1M output tokens; cheaper “mini/fast” variants go down to well under 1 USD per 1M tokens).[^3_5][^3_6][^3_7]


## What to actually use

- For voice: use ElevenLabs Agents or TTS/STT APIs as your audio front‑end and turn‑taking layer; it is specialized, has a drop‑in widget, and is costed by minutes, which maps directly to “click mic and talk” usage.[^3_8][^3_9][^3_1]
- For brain: choose Grok (or another LLM) behind that, since ElevenLabs explicitly passes through LLM costs rather than replacing them, and Grok’s token pricing is competitive with other major LLMs.[^3_10][^3_11][^3_2]

So the combo is: ElevenLabs for mic/voice + Grok as Mr Blue’s reasoning engine; “which is cheaper” is the wrong axis because they solve different layers of your stack.[^3_10][^3_1]
